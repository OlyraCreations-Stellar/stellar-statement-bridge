# Stellar and Drips Wave ecosystem research

**Research date:** 2026-10-28
**Scope:** Public program pages and rules, official Stellar documentation and funding information, plus all repository metadata exposed by the Drips Stellar Wave public repository API. This is discovery research, not a code audit or an endorsement of any listed project.

## Executive findings

- Drips currently reports **842 approved repositories** on the Stellar repositories page. The public API returned 842 records across 43 pages (20 per page, last page partial). The Stellar Wave overview separately reported 843 repos during this research window. These are live counters and may reflect different refresh times; use the repository page/API count as a snapshot, not a durable program statistic.
- The latest displayed cycle, Wave 10, ran **October 15–22, 2026** with a displayed $75,000 budget. The same page said there were no active or upcoming Waves when checked on October 28. Building now cannot be justified by assuming an immediately open payout cycle.
- The approved catalog is crowded across consumer payment apps, escrow, DeFi, RWA/tokenization, wallets, and developer/monitoring tools. Many listings are hackathon projects and multi-repo applications. Category counts derived from short descriptions would be noisy, so this report uses the full catalog to identify clusters and examines named competitors individually.
- Stellar already offers an official disbursement platform (SDP) for bulk payments, including vendor payouts, up to 10,000 recipients per batch, recipient wallet onboarding, real-time payment tracking/analytics, role controls, and accounting-compatible CSV exports. This directly weakens a generic outbound payout/reconciliation app opportunity. A narrow ERP/AP reconciliation connector remains a hypothesis, not a demonstrated gap.
- Repository approval means the program accepted an application; it does not mean SDF/Drips audited, endorsed, funded, or certified a product. Selection is discretionary and can change.
- A plausible gap to validate is **operational exception handling and reconciliation for Stellar-based merchant payouts**: connect an off-chain payable/invoice to a Stellar settlement and give an operator a clear queue for unmatched, late, partial, duplicate, or memo-mismatched payments. Existing systems cover pieces (invoicing, explorers/indexers, anchor transfer protocols), but the end-to-end operator workflow remains unverified.

## Stellar technology and strategic fit

### Relevant network capabilities

- Stellar is a public ledger with native issued assets and smart-contract capabilities through Soroban. Stellar's current developer home describes ledger closes around five seconds, native asset issuer controls, path payments, fee sponsorship, and sponsored reserves. These are general network properties, not a guarantee that every payment corridor has liquidity or fiat access. [Stellar developer home](https://developers.stellar.org/)
- Stellar assets and payment flows can be discoverable through public interfaces such as Horizon and Stellar RPC. Horizon ingests and serves current and historical ledger data, supports streaming, pagination, and has retention/storage considerations for operators. It is an API layer, not a business reconciliation system. [Horizon overview](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/data/apis/horizon) · [Horizon ingestion](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/data/apis/horizon/admin-guide/ingestion) · [Horizon API structure](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/data/apis/horizon/api-reference/structure)
- Soroban is appropriate when a project needs programmable state transitions or contract-enforced authorization/settlement. It is not automatically needed for reading payments, matching invoices, dashboards, or alert delivery. [Smart contract overview](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/smart-contracts/overview)
- Anchors expose standardized asset transfer and authentication flows. SEP-1 publishes integration metadata, SEP-10 authenticates an account, and SEP-24 supports hosted deposit/withdrawal interaction; Anchor Platform can emit callbacks for transaction/KYC status rather than forcing clients to poll. These interfaces do not guarantee that every anchor implements every SEP or corridor. [SEP-1](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/apps/example-application-tutorial/anchor-integration/sep1) · [SEP-10](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/apps/example-application-tutorial/anchor-integration/sep10) · [SEP-24](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/apps/example-application-tutorial/anchor-integration/sep24) · [Anchor Platform events](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/platforms/anchor-platform/admin-guide/events/getting-started)

### SDF programs and ecosystem priorities

- SDF describes its mission as equitable access to the global financial system and identifies everyday financial services, trustworthy technology, open participation, and ecosystem utility as strategic priorities. [SDF Foundation](https://stellar.org/foundation)
- The **Stellar Community Fund (SCF) Build Award** page advertises up to $150,000 in XLM for validated projects transitioning toward launch, with Open, Integration, and RFP tracks. At the research date, the awards page showed SCF #46 submission with a November 8, 2026 deadline and SCF #45 notification/distribution. These are round-specific facts, not guaranteed eligibility or a promise of funding. [SCF Build Awards](https://communityfund.stellar.org/awards) · [SDF grants overview](https://stellar.org/grants-and-funding)
- Academic Research Grants are for eligible academic institutions and principal investigators, with research topics including privacy, formal verification, distributed systems, DeFi market mechanisms, and empirical Stellar studies. This is not a general startup grant. [SDF Academic Research Grants](https://research.stellar.org/research-grants)
- SDF marketing grants target growth/user acquisition for projects with a live product, traction, clear use case, plan, and execution capability; they are not seed funding for an unvalidated idea. [Marketing Grants](https://stellar.org/grants-and-funding/marketing-grants)

### SDF's official disbursement platform as the primary benchmark

The [SDP product page](https://stellar.org/products-and-tools/disbursement-platform) describes bulk disbursements up to 10,000 recipients per batch, recipient wallet onboarding, vendor payouts, real-time payment tracking/analytics, role permissions, and accounting-compatible CSV exports. The [disbursement UI docs](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/platforms/stellar-disbursement-platform/admin-guide/user-interface/disbursements) say the dashboard lists disbursement status and payment counts and can export a CSV matching the current UI filters. The upload API accepts fields including `paymentID`; the dashboard payment page supports search by Payment ID and export, and the [payment export API](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/platforms/stellar-disbursement-platform/api-reference/export-payments-csv) exports payment CSVs. The [disbursement export API](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/platforms/stellar-disbursement-platform/api-reference/export-disbursements) filters disbursement records by status/date. This confirms that a manually exported CSV and a payment-ID matcher alone would be a weak differentiator.

The official repositories [backend](https://github.com/stellar/stellar-disbursement-platform-backend) and [frontend](https://github.com/stellar/stellar-disbursement-platform-frontend) were active in the snapshot (last pushes Oct. 27 and Oct. 26 respectively); the backend is Go and the README links the API reference. A sampled open backend issue (#1184) requests balance tracking and Decaf username transfers, not AP/ERP matching. Public docs reviewed here establish manual CSV/API export and tracking; they do not establish whether the SDP roadmap includes direct ERP integrations or whether users already automate the export/import externally.

The two exact official SDP repository slugs above were not present in the 842 currently listed Drips repository records. This does not imply that an adapter could not be Wave eligible, only that the official SDP repositories themselves were not found under those names in this snapshot.

### Focused integration feasibility: SDP to ERPNext

Official docs now verify a concrete, narrow integration surface:

- SDP's [List All Payments API](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/platforms/stellar-disbursement-platform/api-reference/list-all-payments) is paginated and documents filters for status, type, receiver, and date. Its response schema includes the SDP payment UUID, optional `external_payment_id`, `stellar_transaction_id`, status, amount, asset code/issuer, source wallet, and timestamps. It documents `SUCCESS`, `FAILED`, `PENDING`, and other states.
- SDP's [API Keys guide](https://developers.stellar.org/docs/platforms/stellar-disbursement-platform/api-reference/api-keys) says API keys can have granular read/write permissions and IP restrictions. A connector can be designed around read-only permission, subject to confirming the precise permission scope in the target deployment.
- ERPNext's [Bank Transaction guide](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/bank-transaction) defines the record as statement activity that can be reconciled to vouchers. [Bank Reconciliation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/bank-reconciliation) keeps matching and voucher handling inside ERPNext. The public ERPNext `develop` DocType schema includes `date`, `bank_account`, `company`, `currency`, `description`, `reference_number`, `transaction_id`, `deposit`, and `withdrawal`; this source snapshot must be rechecked against the selected supported ERPNext release before implementation.
- ERPNext's [Payment Entry documentation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry) says submitting a Payment Entry posts General Ledger entries. That makes an importer that creates only Bank Transactions materially safer than automatically posting vouchers, but it does not remove the need for accounting review.
- A read-only public GitHub search on 2026-10-28 found no matching SDP-to-ERPNext repository or issue using the exact search terms tested. Search coverage is incomplete and is not evidence that no private, unindexed, or differently named integration exists.

This supports implementation feasibility, not market demand. The differentiation hypothesis is not a new reconciliation engine: it is a paginated, asset-aware, idempotent import from SDP into ERPNext's existing statement/reconciliation workflow. Native CSV import may already be enough. No operator interviews or user dataset were available at the time of research.

### Technical strengths, weaknesses, and contribution opportunities

The public repository catalog gives enough evidence to say that approved projects span contracts, SDKs, applications, APIs, and indexers, and that several show open issues. It does **not** justify generalized quality ratings. Selected projects were recently pushed to during the snapshot, including RouteDock, Quittance, Stellopay Core, Micopay, Trustless Work, SafeTrust, TalentTrust, Bridgelet, and Sub-Rosa. This is evidence of recent repository activity only, not adoption, stability, or maintainer responsiveness.

Open contribution work is visible on the repository/issue trackers for several projects, but assigning an issue to a new independent product is not a substitute for contributing to an existing one. In particular, before building an indexer, SDK, escrow, invoice app, payment router, or RWA tool, inspect the live code, current issue backlog, release history, architecture, and maintainer willingness to accept collaboration.

## Unverified assumptions and next research gates

1. Whether small businesses or payout operators experience a sufficiently costly Stellar payment reconciliation problem.
2. Whether a buyer/payee can provide a safe pilot dataset or sandbox with invoices, payment identifiers, and correction cases.
3. Whether Quittance, StellarLens, Wraith, Concord, Anchor Platform tooling, or another product already provides the exact exception workflow.
4. Whether the target corridor uses Stellar-issued stablecoins and accessible compliant fiat on/off-ramps. Ledger settlement alone does not establish fiat liquidity or legal availability.
5. Current SCF track eligibility and upcoming deadlines at the time of any application; round state changes.
6. Current Wave approval process and any caps/eligibility changes before applying. Repo approval is discretionary.

## Research artifacts and caveats

- All 842 public approved-repo metadata rows were fetched from the same endpoint used by the Drips public repositories page on Oct. 28, 2026. Individual repo source code was not audited at catalog scale.
